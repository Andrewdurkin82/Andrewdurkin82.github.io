Banner ERP Automation

Keyboard-driven workflow automation for repetitive student ERP work, designed around the reality that enterprise software rarely behaves like the clean demo version.

Automate the repetition. Keep the exceptions visible.

I built hotkey-driven workflows around repetitive Banner student ERP tasks to reduce clicking, navigation, and repeated data entry. The useful part was not simply making the happy path faster. The workflow also had to survive the strange historical records, pop-ups, and interface conditions that appear when real student data has accumulated over time.

One example exposed the difference between a macro and a usable automation: a prior advising record could trigger a pop-up at exactly the point the workflow expected to enter a new value. That forced the automation to account for history and exception states instead of blindly replaying keystrokes.
