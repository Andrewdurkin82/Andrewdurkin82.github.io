01 / PERSONAL AI

Jeppe

A personal AI system designed to know what it knows, where it learned it, and when uncertainty should remain unresolved.

Jeppe combines evidence-backed context, provenance, user correction, bounded retrieval, multi-user separation, provider/model transparency, history import, and systematic retrieval evaluation. It runs as a working system on dedicated hardware rather than living forever in prototype purgatory.

18-Minute OTD

A new target changed the metric. I focused on what needed to change in the workflow.

How I work
